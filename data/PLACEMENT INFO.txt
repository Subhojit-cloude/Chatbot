## PLACEMENT

## HOW TO APPROACH  
 
[1] OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Department: Training & Placement (T&P) Cell
- Core Focus: Lays equal emphasis on both vital functions: Training and Placement.
- Academic Integration: Academic departments and their faculty members are closely associated with the T&P process through formal representation inside the Cell.


[2] THREE CORE COMPONENTS OF TRAINING
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Industrial Exposure:
   - Vocational Trainings
   - Industrial Visits
   - Seminars and Workshops
   - Interactive sessions with Senior Industry Professionals who educate students on advanced technologies and contemporary industrial practices.

2. Finishing School Programme:
   - Polishes soft skills, professional conduct, interview preparation, and corporate readiness.

3. Technology-Based Courses:
   - Systematic brush-up and skill augmentation in relevant technical stacks and core domains.


[3] RESOURCE DELIVERY MECHANISMS
------------------------------------------------------------
The training programs are executed through four primary resource channels:
1. Interventions by External Professionals and Faculty.
2. Interventions by Active Industry Professionals.
3. Interventions by College Faculty (specifically trained to deliver advanced, industry-relevant curriculum).
4. Interactive Webinars.
