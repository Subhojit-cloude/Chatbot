MAKAUT GUIDELINES EXPLAINED: MAR AND MOOCs

---

1. MAR (MANDATORY ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS)

---

What is it?
MAR is a compulsory requirement by MAKAUT. Along with your regular studies and exams, you MUST complete certain extracurricular and co-curricular activities to get your final degree. Even if you pass all your exams, you will not receive your final degree if your MAR points are incomplete.

Points Required:

* For 4-Year B.Tech Students: 100 Points Total (Minimum 25 points per academic year).
* For Lateral Entry (3-Year) B.Tech Students: 75 Points Total (Minimum 25 points per academic year).
* For BBA/BCA/B.Sc (3-Year) Students: 75 Points Total.

How to Earn MAR Points (Examples):

* Tech Fests & Workshops (As a participant or organizer)
* Blood Donation, Tree Plantation, or Relief Camp work
* Short Online Courses / MOOCs
* Publishing Research Papers, Articles, or Wall Magazines
* Self-Entrepreneurship / Start-up Business Plans
* Sports, Yoga Camps, or Cultural events

Important Rules for MAR:

* MAR points do NOT increase your DGPA/SGPA, but they are strictly mandatory for graduation.
* You need to submit certificates or solid proofs of your activities to your college coordinator to get the points approved.
* While the goal is 100 points, MAKAUT prefers you to earn them steadily (about 25 a year) rather than doing them all at the very end.

---

2. MOOCs (MASSIVE OPEN ONLINE COURSES) FOR HONOURS

---

What is it?
Unlike MAR, completing MOOCs for Honours is OPTIONAL. If you want your final degree to say "B.Tech with Honours," you need to complete extra online learning courses outside of your regular college syllabus.

Credits Required for Honours:

* For 4-Year B.Tech Students: 20 Extra Credits.
* For Lateral Entry B.Tech Students: 16 Extra Credits.
* These credits should be distributed over your 4 years (usually 4 to 8 credits per year).

Where to do MOOCs:
You can complete these courses from recognized educational platforms such as:

* SWAYAM / NPTEL (Highly recommended and standard for MAKAUT)
* Coursera
* edX
* Udemy
* Simplilearn

How Credits are Calculated (General Guideline):

* 4 to 7 weeks course = 1 Credit
* 8 to 11 weeks course = 2 Credits
* 12 to 15 weeks course = 3 Credits
* 16 weeks or more = 4 Credits
(If the course duration is in hours, every 8 hours roughly equals 1 Credit).

Important Rules for MOOCs:

* No Double Dipping: The MOOCs you use to claim "Honours credits" CANNOT be used again to claim "MAR points." You must do separate courses if you want both.
* Always ensure you finish the course and receive the final completion certificate, as you will need to submit this to your college to claim the credits.

---

SUMMARY FOR STUDENTS:

* MAR = MANDATORY. You must do extracurriculars or short courses to earn 100 points just to graduate.
* MOOCs = OPTIONAL. You earn 20 credits of heavy online courses only if you want an "Honours" tag on your final degree.
