[1] OVERVIEW OF STUDENT CLUBS & ACTIVITIES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Extracurricular and co-curricular student-driven bodies promoting technical innovation, cultural arts, social outreach, entrepreneurship, and creative expression across the campus.
- Supervision: Governed under the student affairs and institutional development bodies with faculty advisors and student office bearers.


[2] KEY ACTIVE STUDENT CLUBS & CHAPTERS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. TECHNICAL & INNOVATION CLUBS:
   - Coding Club / GDSC (Google Developer Student Club): Focuses on competitive programming, web/app development, open-source projects, and technical hackathons.
   - Robotics Club: Organizes workshops and competitions on autonomous/manual bots, embedded systems, IoT, and mechanical design.
   - AEC Innovation & Incubation Council (IIC) / EDC (Entrepreneurship Development Cell): Fosters start-up ideas, incubation support, business pitch sessions, and intellectual property/patent guidance.

2. CULTURAL & CREATIVE CLUBS:
   - Cultural Club: Organizes the annual cultural fest, musical performances, dance events, dramatic productions, and inter-college cultural competitions.
   - Music & Fine Arts Society: Encourages instrumental, vocal, painting, sketching, and digital arts across campus batches.
   - Photography & Media Club: Documents campus events, conducts photography walks, video editing workshops, and digital media production.
   - Literary & Debating Society: Organizes debate sessions, elocution contests, model UNs, creative writing, and annual magazine publications.

3. SPORTS & SOCIAL OUTREACH:
   - Sports Club: Organizes inter-departmental cricket, football, volleyball, badminton tournaments, and annual athletic meets.
   - NSS (National Service Scheme) Unit: Drives community service, blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, and health/hygiene awareness in adopted rural areas.


[3] MAJOR ANNUAL EVENTS HOSTED BY CLUBS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Annual Technical Fest: Inter-college technical symposium featuring coding challenges, robo-wars, paper presentations, and tech exhibits.
- Annual Cultural Fest: Flagship multi-day cultural festival featuring celebrity performances, battle of the bands, dance showcases, and theater.
- Annual Sports Meet: Multi-sport track and field, indoor, and outdoor team athletic events.
