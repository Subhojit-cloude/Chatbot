## Hostel Facilities & Amenities
- Accommodation: AC and non-AC rooms available; spacious double-seated layouts
- Segregation: Separate dedicated hostel for 1st-year students to prevent ragging
- Dining: Full daily meal service (Breakfast, Lunch, Tiffin, Dinner)
- Utilities & Connectivity: High-speed Wi-Fi campus, 24x7 electricity, 24x7 water facility, geysers in bathrooms, purified drinking water
- Safety & Security: 24x7 CCTV surveillance, round-the-clock security personnel, active Anti-Ragging Squad, continuous presence of Hostel Superintendents
- Healthcare: Emergency medical support and an emergency vehicle for medical transit
- Recreation & Study: Dedicated hostel library, well-equipped gymnasium, indoor and outdoor sports facilities
- Community: Extracurricular and social activities (e.g., tree plantation drives, blood donation camps)
