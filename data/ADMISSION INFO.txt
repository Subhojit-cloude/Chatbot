Program 1: B.Tech Degree (4-Year Course)
Eligibility Criteria: Passed 10+2 examination.
Required Subjects: Any three from Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Electronics, Information Technology, Biology, Informatics Practices, Biotechnology, Technical Vocational subject, Agriculture, Engineering Graphics, Business Studies, or Entrepreneurship.
Minimum Marks: 45% aggregate in the above three subjects (40% for reserved categories).
Admission Procedure: Based on merit ranks secured in WBJEE (West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination) or JEE (Mains).
Counselling Portals: www.wbjeeb.in / www.wbjeeb.nic.in
Final Admission: Campus reporting for formalities, admission kit, and fee payment after seat allotment.

Program 2: B.Tech Degree (3-Year Lateral Entry Course)
Entry Level: Admission directly to the 2nd year (3rd semester).
Eligibility Criteria: 
- Option A: Diploma in Engineering/Technology/Pharmacy from an AICTE recognized institute with at least 60% overall marks.
- Option B: B.Sc. Degree from a UGC-approved university with at least 60% marks AND passed XII standard with Mathematics.
Admission Procedure: Must qualify in the JELET written test conducted by the West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board.
Counselling Portal: www.wbjeeb.in
