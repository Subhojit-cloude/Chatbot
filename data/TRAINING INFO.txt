## VALUE ADDED COURSE

[1] OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Value Added Courses (VAC) play a vital role in enhancing student employability and broadening skill sets in specific technical or professional areas.
- Nature: Part of the "Beyond-curriculum" initiative designed to equip students with practical skills needed for professional success.
- Instructors: Conducted by experienced professionals.
- Course Design Process: Feedback from alumni, current students, faculty members/academicians, and industry employers is analyzed to identify curriculum gaps before the course structure is finalized.
- Approval Workflow: Developed and approved by the Departmental Academic Committee (DAC), then placed before the Academic Committee for final institutional approval.


[2] ELIGIBILITY, DURATION & PASSING CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------
- Course Duration: At least 30 hours for every Value Added Course.
- Registration / Enrollment: Students must enroll under the supervision of the Departmental VAC Coordinator or Head of the Department (HOD).
- Minimum Attendance Required: At least 80% attendance.
- Minimum Marks Required: At least 60% marks in the assessment.
- Completion Condition: Students must satisfy both the 80% attendance and 60% score criteria to pass and receive course completion.
